## Exercise data and licence

Exercise data (names, descriptions, muscle groups and equipment) comes from the
[wger Workout Manager](https://wger.de), a free and open-source fitness project.
Each exercise is shared by its wger contributors under a Creative Commons licence:
most under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), some under
[CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/), and a few dedicated to the
public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

RepRadar adapts this data: descriptions are converted to plain text and shortened, and
wger's muscles and equipment are mapped to RepRadar's own categories. In keeping with the
ShareAlike terms, the adapted exercise data is shared under CC BY-SA 4.0. This applies to
the exercise data only, not to RepRadar's source code.
